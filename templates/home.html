Engineered for Flawless Operations

Every module is designed to eliminate guesswork, enforce physical safety constraints, and automate fleet accounting.

Intelligent Payload Validation

Real-time client and server checks prevent assigning cargo loads greater than a vehicle's maximum rated capacity ($\text{Weight} \le \text{MaxCap}$).

Maintenance Auto-Lock

Logging any preventative service automatically shifts the asset status to In Shop, removing it from dispatcher pools until certified complete.

Driver Compliance & Safety

License expiration alerts, category compatibility matching (Truck vs. Van vs. Bike), and automated safety scores ensure zero regulatory fines.

Automated ROI & Cost-per-KM

Live accounting aggregating fuel liters, maintenance invoices, and road expenses to calculate precise vehicle profitability and fuel economy ($\text{km/L}$).

End-to-End Trip Lifecycle

Manage trips across Draft $\rightarrow$ Dispatched $\rightarrow$ Completed states with starting & final odometer verification.

One-Click Audits & Exports

Generate downloadable CSV datasets and formatted printable reports for monthly payroll, financial audits, and safety inspections.
